Food Cuisine Meal Dataset — 1,200 records

Coverage: 6 cuisines × 4 meal types × 50 records.
Cuisines: Indian, Arabic, Chinese, American, Mexican, Thai.

The 50 records per cuisine/meal are 25 named dishes with two labels: Traditional and Home-style. These labels are catalog variants, not claims that recipes or nutrition values are identical.

Nutrition fields are intentionally blank and marked Not yet verified. Populate them only after defining recipe ingredients and serving mass, then map ingredients to a trusted nutrient database such as USDA FoodData Central or a regionally appropriate source.

This dataset is suitable as a food-catalog seed for annotation, image search, and schema development; it is not a validated clinical nutrition database.
